# Third-party notices

## SDHLT

Blockout supports SDHLT v1.2.0 by seedee. Compiler executables are deliberately
not stored in this repository or its tester package. `Setup Blockout.cmd`
downloads the official release and verifies the published package used during
development with recorded SHA-256 hashes.

- Project: https://github.com/seedee/SDHLT
- Release: https://github.com/seedee/SDHLT/releases/tag/v1.2.0
- Source and hashes: `tools/SDHLT_SOURCE.txt`
- Upstream terms: `tools/SDHLT_LICENSE.md`
- Upstream readme: `tools/SDHLT_README.md`

SDHLT and Blockout are separate programs. SDHLT is not covered by Blockout's
MIT license.

## Material previews

The `BO_` material library consists of 43 CC0 surfaces. Exact source pages,
authors, and download URLs are recorded in
`textures/asset-manifest.base.json` and `textures/ASSET_SOURCES.md`.

The `SUN_` material set is original Blockout project artwork. Seven material
names correspond to textures supplied by a user's legal Half-Life or
Counter-Strike installation. Their repository miniatures are original
procedural SVG placeholders; Valve texture pixels are not included.
